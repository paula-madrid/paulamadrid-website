Sol & Mar

  • Graphic Identity
  • Logo Design

Year: 2025
Client: Sol & Mar Inmobiliaria

The identity for Sol & Mar, a real estate agency based on the Catalan coast, draws inspiration from the Mediterranean landscape: the meeting of sun and sea. The design system combines bold, modern typography with a fresh color palette of deep blue marines and coral accents. This visual language aims to reflect both lifestyle and trust: approachable, vibrant, and contemporary.
