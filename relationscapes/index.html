Relationscapes

  • Illustration

Year: 2024
Client: Blair Hodges

For my first-ever podcast cover design, I had the privilege of creating the artwork for Relationscapes, a show by Blair Hodges that explores the intricate landscapes of human connections. The illustration depicts a planet with different people constellations sitting on it, symbolizing the diverse and complex ways in which we form relationships. The design aims to reflect the beauty of human connection in all its forms, using vibrant, harmonious colors to bring the concept to life and invite listeners into a world of diverse emotional landscapes.
