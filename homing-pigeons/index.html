Homing Pigeons

  • Illustration

Year: 2021
Client: Passion Project

I designed this set of eight postage stamps featuring homing pigeons—palomas mensajeras in Spanish. Known for their incredible ability to find their way home, these birds were once vital carriers of written messages. Each stamp highlights a different pigeon, capturing its unique personality while honoring their historical role in communication. The illustrations are done in clean lines and a simple, cartoon style to suit the small format of the stamps.
