Marijane

  • Graphic Identity
  • Logo Design

Year: 2025
Client: Marijane

I had the pleasure of working with Beata on the rebranding of her CBD shop in Wrocław, Poland. The goal was to move away from stereotypical cannabis branding and create a visual identity that felt fresh, feminine, and approachable.

The scope included logo design and color direction. We explored several illustrated logo options before landing on a playful yet serene character to represent marijane. I developed a bright, warm color palette to reflect the brand’s positive, feel-good vibe.
